## Decision changes
1. Keep Brand (`Power Every Move`), PMM (M3's choice reason) and Campaign (`Make Room for What Matters`) separate.
2. Define the audience and task as a campaign communication **hypothesis**, not proof of market demand.
3. Compare **task alternatives** — manual flat mop, wet/dry floor cleaning appliance, comparable electric mop — without invented superiority.
4. Trace three product-value pillars through FABE and RTB; photographs are structural evidence, not performance testing. Any runtime, ergonomic, floor compatibility or speed claim requires model/version matched support.
5. Present four **message-design states** rather than labeling actual customers without behavioral evidence.
6. Gate commercial CTA on approved SKU, kit, local price, inventory, delivery, warranty, returns and page/offer consistency.
7. Keep evidence/test/owner-specific process in this QA file, not in the public portfolio page.
